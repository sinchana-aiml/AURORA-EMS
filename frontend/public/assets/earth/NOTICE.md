# Earth texture attribution

These locally bundled 2048 × 1024 textures are derived from NASA Blue Marble imagery.

- `nasa-blue-marble-day-2048.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble cloud-free Earth color map.
- `nasa-blue-marble-clouds-2048.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble cloud composite used only as a visual cloud-alpha layer, not as live weather data.

Source imagery and credit: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / NASA Earth Observatory; Blue Marble imagery by Reto Stöckli, with enhancements by Robert Simmon. NASA SVS identifies the Blue Marble Next Generation imagery as NASA/GSFC and NASA Earth Observatory material. NASA imagery is public-domain U.S. government work unless otherwise noted; no NASA logo or endorsement is used.

Download provenance: locally optimized public copies from `Depal-Jakub/orbital-bloom`, which records the original NASA source locations and the 2048 × 1024 dimensions for both files.
